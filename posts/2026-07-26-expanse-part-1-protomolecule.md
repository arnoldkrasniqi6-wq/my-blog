The universe of ***The Expanse*** is one of the most meticulously realized worlds in modern science fiction. Combining hard physics, gritty geopolitics, and cosmic horror, it depicts a future where humanity has colonized the solar system but brought its ancient divisions along for the ride. 

In this first part of our storyline explanation, we will break down the events of **Seasons 1 through 3** (corresponding to the first three novels: *Leviathan Wakes*, *Caliban's War*, and *Abaddon's Gate*).

---

## The Three Factions

By the 23rd century, humanity is split into three main geopolitical blocks:
1. **United Nations (UN) of Earth and Luna**: The old superpower. Earth is overpopulated, with billions living on "Basic Assistance," yet it retains massive military might and historical prestige.
2. **Martian Congressional Republic (MCR)**: A highly advanced, militaristic society dedicated to a single, generational goal—terraforming Mars into a living world. Mars is a rising power with superior technology.
3. **Outer Planets Alliance (OPA) / Belters**: The working class of the solar system. Belters live and work in the low-gravity stations of the Asteroid Belt and Outer Planets, mining the resources that sustain Earth and Mars. They are physically altered by gravity and exploited by the "Inners," leading to deep-seated resentment and radical insurgencies.

---

## The Inciting Incident: The Canterbury and the Scopuli

The story begins with a missing girl, **Julie Mao**, a wealthy Earth heiress who joined the Belter revolutionary cause. Her ship, the *Scopuli*, is boarded by a mysterious stealth vessel.

Meanwhile, the ice-hauler *Canterbury* receives a distress signal from the *Scopuli*. Executive officer **James Holden** leads a small shuttle crew—**Naomi Nagata**, **Amos Burton**, **Alex Kamal**, and **Shed Garvey**—to investigate. While they are aboard the shuttle, a fleet of high-tech stealth ships attacks and vaporizes the *Canterbury*, killing everyone else.

Assuming the stealth ships belong to Mars, Holden broadcasts a message detailing the attack to the entire system. This spark ignites a cold war, bringing Earth and Mars to the brink of open conflict.

---

## The Mystery of Ceres and Julie Mao

On Ceres Station, a cynical Belter detective named **Joe Miller** is hired by his corrupt security agency, Star Helix, to find Julie Mao and return her to her wealthy father on Earth. 

Miller's investigation leads him to discover that Julie was caught up in something far larger than OPA politics. His search intersects with Holden and the surviving crew of the *Canterbury*, who have claimed a Martian gunship, renaming it the **Rocinante** (or *Roci*).

Together, they track Julie Mao's coordinates to the asteroid station **Eros**.

---

## The Tragedy of Eros and the Protomolecule

On Eros, Holden and Miller find Julie Mao's corpse in a hotel room, covered in strange, glowing blue filaments. 

Julie was infected by the **Protomolecule**—an ancient alien construct sent toward Earth billions of years ago from outside the solar system, captured by Saturn's gravity to become the moon Phoebe. The corporate giant *Protogen*, contracted by Julie’s father Jules-Pierre Mao, sought to weaponize the Protomolecule. To observe it in action, they release it onto the civilian population of Eros, locking down the station and exposing its 100,000 residents.

The Protomolecule feeds on radiation and organic matter, absorbing the consciousnesses of the victims. Eros becomes a giant, living biological computer. 

In a terrifying demonstration of physics-defying technology, Eros breaks orbit and begins accelerating directly toward Earth. Detective Miller boards the asteroid, carrying a nuclear bomb, only to find Julie Mao's consciousness merged with the Protomolecule core. He manages to convince her to steer Eros away from Earth, crashing it instead into Venus.

---

## The Ganymede Incident and Caliban Monsters

Following the crash on Venus, the Protomolecule begins building a massive, incomprehensible structure. Meanwhile, Jules-Pierre Mao moves his experiments to Ganymede Station, creating hybrid human-Protomolecule soldiers known as the **Caliban Project**.

When a hybrid is tested on Ganymede, it sparks a disastrous firefight between Earth and Martian forces, devastating the station. Holden and the *Roci* crew work to expose Mao's conspiracy, allying with Martian marine **Bobbie Draper** and UN politician **Chrisjen Avasarala**. Avasarala successfully exposes the rogue elements within the UN government, including Undersecretary Sadavir Errinwright, who was colluding with Mao.

---

## The Ring Gate Opens

On Venus, the Protomolecule structure launches into space, assembling itself into a gargantuan, circular gateway: **The Ring**.

This triggers a race between Earth, Mars, and the OPA. All three factions send fleets to secure the Ring. When a Belter racer recklessly flies through the Ring and decelerates too quickly, he is vaporized, revealing that the Ring Space (the "Slow Zone") has a universal speed limit enforced by an alien station at the center.

Holden, guided by a vision of Detective Miller (acting as a Protomolecule projection or "the Investigator"), enters the alien station. He learns that the creators of the Protomolecule—the **Ring Builders**—were wiped out billions of years ago by an unknown enemy. 

After a tense standoff inside the Ring Space, Holden convinces the human fleets to shut down their ships' reactors, proving they are not a threat to the station. In response, the Ring Space opens up, connecting the solar system to **1,373 new star systems**, each possessing habitable worlds.

Humanity is no longer bound to a single star. The gold rush of the Ring Gates is about to begin.
