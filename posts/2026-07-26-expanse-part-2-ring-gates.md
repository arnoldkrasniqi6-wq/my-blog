With the opening of the 1,373 Ring Gates, the solar system changed forever. The balance of power between Earth, Mars, and the Belt collapsed overnight. 

In this second part of our storyline breakdown, we explore the events of **Seasons 4 through 6** (corresponding to books 4 through 6: *Cibola Burn*, *Nemesis Games*, and *Babylon's Ashes*).

---

## The Gold Rush and the Colonization of Ilus

The immediate consequence of the Ring Gates was a massive wave of colonization. The first colony is established on **Ilus** (named *New Terra* by the Earth corporation chartering it) by Belter refugees fleeing Ganymede. 

When a corporate science vessel representing *Royal Charter Energy (RCE)* attempts to land, Belter extremists blow up their landing pad, killing several people. **James Holden** and the crew of the *Roci* are sent by UN Secretary-General Chrisjen Avasarala to act as neutral mediators.

The conflict on the ground between corporate security chief **Adolphus Murtry** and the Belter refugees quickly turns bloody. However, the true threat is the planet itself. Ilus is an ancient, dormant Ring Builder machine. When Holden's presence inadvertently activates the planet’s defense systems, a massive global disaster is triggered. An explosion destroys the main camp, causing toxic rain, blinding storms, and a flesh-eating parasite outbreak.

Ultimately, detective Miller (functioning as the Protomolecule projection) locates a localized weapon that destroys the active Protomolecule networks on the planet, shutting down the defense systems and saving the remaining survivors.

---

## The Decline of Mars and the Rise of Marco Inaros

The Ring Gates solved humanity’s land crisis but created an existential crisis for Mars. The dream of a terraformed Mars died instantly: why spend generations working to live under a dome when thousands of habitable worlds are free for the taking? Martian society began to unravel, leading to high unemployment, military budget cuts, and widespread corruption. 

Seeing an opportunity, a radical Belter faction leader named **Marco Inaros** emerges. Inaros is charismatic, manipulative, and fiercely nationalistic. He forms the **Free Navy**, uniting disparate radical Belter cells under his leadership.

Inaros makes a secret deal with rogue Martian military factions led by **Admiral Winston Duarte**. In exchange for Martian warships, advanced stealth technology, and military equipment, Inaros provides Duarte with a stolen sample of the Protomolecule.

---

## The Asteroid Attack: Earth’s Darkest Day

Using Martian military-grade carbon stealth coating, Marco Inaros paints several massive asteroids. Because the stealth coating prevents Earth's early-warning satellite networks from detecting them, the asteroids are launched directly into Earth's gravity well without warning.

Three massive stealth asteroids strike Earth, vaporizing cities, causing massive tsunamis, and throwing the planet's atmosphere into a nuclear winter. Millions die instantly, and billions more face starvation as Earth's agriculture and infrastructure collapse.

Simultaneously, Inaros' agents launch coordinated strikes across the system. They assassinate the UN leadership, kidnap Holden's former lover Naomi Nagata's son **Filip**, and attempt to assassinate Chrisjen Avasarala.

---

## The Siege of the Ring Space

Marco Inaros declares the Outer Planets and the Ring Gates as the sole property of the Belt, naming himself the leader of the new Belter empire. 

The *Rocinante* crew is separated during these attacks but eventually reunites. Naomi escapes captivity on Marco’s flagship, while Amos manages to escape Earth after surviving the asteroid impacts. 

A joint fleet of loyal Earth and Martian forces, allied with OPA moderates led by **Camina Drummer**, fights back against the Free Navy. In a desperate final battle, the allied forces attempt to take back the **Medina Station** (the OPA-controlled station located inside the Ring Space). 

When Marco Inaros attempts to send his fleet through the Ring to flank the allies, Naomi uses her knowledge of the Ring's physics. She calculates that sending a massive amount of energy/mass through the Ring at once will trigger the "Ring Entities"—invisible forces that live between universes. The entities wake up, consuming Marco Inaros and his entire fleet, vaporizing them inside the Ring.

---

## The Aftermath: A New Coalition

With the Free Navy defeated, a new political structure is created to ensure lasting peace. The **Transport Union** is formed to manage trade through the Ring Gates, with Camina Drummer appointed as its first leader. 

However, the peace is fragile. The rogue Martian fleet, led by Winston Duarte, has escaped through the **Laconia Gate**. They have locked the gate behind them, taking the Protomolecule and the secret of alien shipbuilding technology with them.
