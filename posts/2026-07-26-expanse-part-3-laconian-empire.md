While the television adaptation of *The Expanse* concluded with Season 6, the story written by James S.A. Corey continues for three more novels: *Persepolis Rising*, *Tiamat's Wrath*, and *Leviathan Falls*. 

In this final part of our storyline explanation, we will dive into the climax of the saga—spanning a 30-year time jump, the rise of an immortal empire, and the final struggle against the forces that wiped out the Ring Builders.

---

## The Rise of the Laconian Empire

Thirty years have passed since the defeat of Marco Inaros. The solar system has enjoyed a period of relative peace and prosperity under the Transport Union. The crew of the *Rocinante* has grown older but continues to run freelance security operations.

Meanwhile, on the planet **Laconia**, the rogue Martian Admiral **Winston Duarte** has spent decades building a highly disciplined, totalitarian empire. Using the stolen Protomolecule sample and ancient orbital construction platforms left behind by the Ring Builders, Duarte designs war vessels that combine human engineering with alien technology. These ships possess self-healing hulls and devastating magnetic beam weapons.

Duarte has also infected himself with a modified version of the Protomolecule, stopping his aging process and giving him telepathic control over Laconian systems, aiming to become an immortal god-emperor.

In a swift and overwhelming invasion, the Laconian Empire sends a single battleship, the *Heart of the Tempest*, through the Ring. It decimates the combined fleets of Earth, Mars, and the Transport Union, forcing humanity to submit to Duarte's rule.

---

## The Goths and the Bullet

As Duarte expands his control, he faces a far greater threat: the **Ring Entities** (often called the "Goths" by fans). 

These entities exist in a dimension adjacent to our own. The Ring Network operates by drawing energy from their universe. Every time humanity sends ships through the Ring Gates, it causes "noise" or "friction" in the Goths' dimension. When the energy load exceeds a certain threshold (called "going Dutchman"), the Goths retaliate.

Their weapons are terrifying: they can temporarily shut down human consciousness system-wide, causing everyone in the galaxy to experience a temporary blackout. 

Duarte decides to wage a preemptive war against the Goths. He sends a ship loaded with antimatter through a gate and detonates it, attempting to "bomb" their dimension. The Goths retaliate by placing a massive, dark sphere—an **alien bullet**—directly inside the Laconian flagship, wiping out the consciousness of everyone on board.

The Goth attacks grow more frequent and severe, threatening to dissolve the physical boundaries of our universe and extinguish all human life.

---

## The Fall of Duarte and the Hive Mind

Under the influence of the Goths' attacks, Duarte's mind is shattered. He becomes a vegetable, but his brain is still connected to the Protomolecule network. 

Before his mind breaks completely, Duarte attempts to use the Protomolecule to link all human brains into a single, galaxy-wide hive mind. He believes that if humanity is united as a single consciousness, they can withstand the Goth attacks. In reality, this would destroy human individuality and free will forever.

Holden, Naomi, Amos, and their allies organize a final resistance. They locate Duarte on the alien station at the center of the Ring Space (the Slow Zone). **Amos Burton**, who was killed on Laconia but resurrected by the planet's repair drones (making him semi-immortal and partially connected to the network), helps the crew navigate the alien station.

---

## James Holden’s Ultimate Sacrifice

On the alien station, Holden finds Duarte attempting to merge with the Ring Station's core. Holden kills Duarte to stop the hive mind from taking over humanity. 

However, the threat of the Goths remains. The Goths are actively tearing down the physical laws of the Ring Space, and within hours, they will destroy every human star system connected to the network.

Holden realizes there is only one way to save humanity. He injects himself with a massive dose of the Protomolecule, allowing him to temporarily interface with the Ring Station. 

Using his control over the station, Holden holds the gateway open long enough for the human fleets inside the Ring Space to escape back to their respective star systems. Once the Ring Space is cleared of all ships, **Holden destroys the Ring Station from the inside**.

---

## The End of the Rings

The destruction of the Ring Station collapses the Ring Space and shuts down all 1,373 Ring Gates forever. 

Humanity is instantly severed. The colonies are isolated, left to survive on their own across the galaxy. Earth, Mars, and the Belt are cut off from the worlds they colonized. 

Though isolated, humanity survives. The threat of the Goths is ended because the energy siphon from their dimension is gone. 

### Epilogue: One Thousand Years Later

A thousand years after the collapse of the Ring Gates, scientists on a distant colony planet succeed in developing a new form of interstellar travel that does not rely on the Ring Space (using a form of warp drive instead). 

They build a ship and fly back to Sol system to see what became of Earth. Upon arriving, they are greeted by a semi-immortal, cybernetic **Amos Burton**, who has survived on Earth for a millennium, watching over the slowly rebuilding cradle of humanity. 

*“Hey,”* Amos says. *“Welcome back.”*
